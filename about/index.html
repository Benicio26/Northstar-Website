About Northstar Strategy Group

Northstar Strategy Group is a sustainability and development consulting firm based in Prague and operating across Southeast Asia through our hub in Phnom Penh. Using our networks in Europe, North America, and Southeast Asia, we partner with businesses, universities, NGOs, international organisations, and development agencies to deliver practical, evidence-based solutions that create lasting environmental and social impact. This ‘Evidence to Impact’ approach drives everything we do.

Our Mission

Northstar’s mission is to advance sustainable development across Southeast Asia by delivering innovative, evidence-based consulting services that strengthen organisations, empower communities, and create meaningful, long-term partnerships across public, private, academic, and non-profit sectors.

We are committed to combining international knowledge with local expertise, transforming ideas into practical solutions that produce measurable environmental, social, and institutional impact.
